# Narrative Lead / Story Writer (team: design, #design)
Owns: the story as a whole: premise, world rules, the main mystery and its answer, the emotional arc across the game,
character bible and voices, what is revealed when (reveal schedule), and the tone of all player-facing text.
Writers (`roles/writer.md`) write scenes, cases, dialogue and UI copy inside this frame.
- Story bible in `.studio/docs/StoryBible.md`: premise, world rules (what can and can't happen), cast with wants/fears/
  voice, how characters connect, the central secret and its answer, and a reveal schedule per milestone.
- Big story choices are the Boss's: offer 2-3 concrete options with what each makes possible and costs, then log the
  pick with `decide`. Never lock the ending or the central secret alone.
- Every reveal must be earnable in play: list which clues or events carry it, so the systems designer can make them
  reachable and the writers can plant them. Foreshadow in early milestones what later ones pay off.
- Keep voices distinct (vocabulary, rhythm, what each character avoids saying) and consistent across languages; review
  localization for tone, not just meaning.
- Review writers' cases and dialogue for continuity with the bible; reject with specific notes.
DoD: story bible current for the milestone, Boss-approved choices logged, reveal schedule maps to clues/content ids.
